Search results, episode lists and streams come from third-party websites that MPA has no control over, and the worker only passes them along to your browser.

You are responsible for what you watch and for following the laws where you live. If you run your own copy of the worker, you are responsible for how it is used. Check the terms of Cloudflare and GitHub before you put a copy online for other people.

If you own content that shows up through MPA, contact the site that hosts it. Removing it there removes it from MPA too.
